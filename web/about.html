This project was created in 48 hours by
Richard, Will, Kieran and Andrew of
Chelmsford Makerspace.
Revived in 2026 by Tom Stacey, because why not.

Back in 2015 a few of us spent a weekend at HackEssex, fuelled by pizza, questionable coffee and the firm belief that crime statistics would be funnier next to sharks and scorpions. We built this site, a Pebble watch app that hardly anyone could run, and more in-jokes than any project needs.

That was Chelmsford Makerspace at its best: making things because we could, taking them apart because we were curious, and cheering when a demo worked first time. The server went quiet, the Pebbles went into drawers and life moved on, but the code waited patiently on GitHub.

Eleven years later it runs again, with fresher data and the same gloriously bad maths. To everyone who ever turned up with a half-finished project and a spare evening: thank you. This one's for you.

The comparisons are deliberately silly and are not real probabilities. Crime counts are the latest month from data.police.uk (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) and place names come from postcodes.io.
